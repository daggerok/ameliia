## Math Quest (current version, v20261021-quest)

A game for a 4th grader in New Jersey with Lina (a girl in glasses) and Pip the capybara (a new hobby every time she shows up), in English and Russian. The daily goal is right answers, not time: while a homework is current (from the day it was given to its last day, `HOMEWORKS` in `src/engine/game.ts`) it is a plan that covers every kind of task on its photos (the Topic 3 test prep up to 2026-10-23: one right answer for each of its 48 kinds; Lesson 4-2 from 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-30: 7 kinds, three right answers each on the day it was given and one on each repeat day; the homework button and normal play serve the kinds not covered yet, never a repeat), otherwise 30 right answers a day; a live clock shows today's study time:

- the order of tasks in Play (owner, 2026-10-26): the homework of the week first, always, until the day's plan is covered; then a failed skill (theory, quiz, a new task); then old skills whose review day has come; then free practice, where learning time is split 50% division, 25% motion and speed, 25% rounding and estimating
- spaced review (`level`, `dueOn` per skill in `src/engine/game.ts`): a right answer on or after the due day raises the level by one and schedules the next review that many days later (1, 2, 3, 4 ... days), extra right answers before the day change nothing, a wrong answer drops the level to 0 and makes the skill due today; a day without a homework is a review day: the goal is 30 right answers with the reviews due as rows, and Progress shows the next review date and the interval of every skill under the skill
- a wrong answer opens a theory page (with fresh examples and her own task step by step), then a mini quiz; after the quiz comes a new task of the same skill, and the skill returns a few tasks later
- tasks follow NJSLS grade 4: 4.NBT.B.6 division up to 4-digit by 1-digit with remainders, 4.OA.A.3 interpreting remainders and reasonableness, 4.NBT.A.3 rounding, 4.NBT.B.5 multiplication, 4.M.A.1-2 time and distance, 4-PS3-1 and 4-PS3-3 speed and energy; velocity and acceleration are bonus explorer words
- a welcome tour on the first visit (again after a reset or from Settings), a daily practice test card, the same test as the Practice test button (every kind of task of every homework of the week exactly once, 31 on 2026-10-29 for Lessons 4-2 to 4-5, the whole last week on a day without homework, plus one task of every old skill due for a review that day, at most 5; owner's rules of 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-29, the old fixed mix of 19 tasks stays in `TEST_PLAN` for a test without a day), stars that paint a magic picture with a congratulation and her own choice of the next one (6 pictures), a story book that opens chapter by chapter, a Progress screen for Mom and Dad (next bullet)
- Progress (for Mom and Dad): Today, 7 days or All time; minutes, tasks, right, wrong, hints; every skill expands into its kinds of tasks with asked, right, wrong, the last day and which kinds were never asked (the homework kinds show today's need, `KINDS_OF` and `kindName` in `src/engine/kinds.ts`); a task log with the task, her answer and the right answer, a mistakes-only filter and where the task came from (play, homework, rework, test); a by-day table and the practice tests with the skills missed; the rollups are pure functions in `src/engine/progress.ts`. The log keeps the last 1,000 tasks (`HISTORY_MAX`); tasks answered before this update show only their skill
- progress is saved on the device in localStorage with a copy in IndexedDB (the newer one wins), persistent storage is requested, and Settings can save or load progress as a file

- "Fix my mistakes": her 18 homework mistakes of 2026-10-22 (her answer, the right way, a rule), then every wrong answer in the app (tasks and practice tests, last 60); each one is reviewed, then new tasks of the same kind until one is right; the button opens a list by topic (since 2026-10-29): start all in random order, fix all mistakes of one topic in a row, or open a topic and pick one mistake to fix first; old mistakes also come back by themselves in Play and test prep (1-3 waiting: every 5th task, 4-10: every 4th, more: every 3rd), and after a wrong answer the app says what most likely went wrong (a lost zero, a basic fact, a misread factor, a carry, swapped digits, rounding)
- homework of 2026-10-26, Topic 4 Lesson 4-2 "Use models to multiply 2-digit numbers by multiples of 10" (`src/engine/topic4.ts`, Additional Practice 4-2 p. 45-46): arrays and area models (10 × 12, 20 × 18), products like 50 × 15, equal groups in a story (30 floors of 18 feet), how many more minutes in a month (Greg and Marta), two kinds of boxes (the dentist, Mrs. Harrigan), which product is greater without multiplying and Miranda's claim; the home screen homework button names the current homework and the daily plan repeats the lesson until Friday 2026-10-30
- homework of 2026-10-27, Topic 4 Lesson 4-3 "Estimate: use rounding or compatible numbers" (`src/engine/topic4est.ts`, skill `est-2x2`, Additional Practice 4-3 p. 47-48): 23 × 12 with "23 is close to 25, 12 is close to __" on a sheet, estimate each product by rounding to tens, more than one way to estimate (select all), the bathtub for 31 days, gadgets for 4 weeks (weeks into days first), the vocabulary term compatible numbers, greater or less than the actual product, rounding or compatible numbers, two estimates with numbers from the tour guide's box; three right answers per kind on 2026-10-27, one on each day until Friday 2026-10-30. The newest homework of the week comes first in Play and on the homework button, the older one after it is covered for the day (`uncoveredFirst`)
- homework of 2026-10-28, Topic 4 Lesson 4-4 "Arrays and Partial Products" (`src/engine/topic4arr.ts`, skill `mult-2x2`, Additional Practice 4-4 p. 49-50, NJSLS 4.NBT.B.5): 12 × 24 as an array cut into tens and ones with four partial products (problems 1-4), Barb's hours each week for 14 weeks, is Teri's answer for 4,296 × 7 reasonable, the total sales of two kinds of boxes at Nolan's store, the missing partial products and the missing factor from a box; three right answers per kind on 2026-10-28, one on each day until Friday 2026-10-30, and it comes before Lessons 4-3 and 4-2 that day. Its theory is written for a 9-year-old: short lines, a chocolate bar cut into 4 pieces, and a worked example for every kind of task
- homework of 2026-10-29, Topic 4 Lesson 4-5 "Area Models and Partial Products" (`src/engine/topic4area.ts`, skill `dist-2x2`, Additional Practice 4-5 p. 51-52, NJSLS 4.NBT.B.5): a drawn area model to fill in (14 × 19, 21 × 51), 23 × 18 = (20 + 3) × (10 + 8) with the Distributive Property, draw an area model and find each product (10 × 18 ... 29 × 64), Ms. Langley's class, the concert and the rodeo, about how much the ferry collected, are Mr. Buckham's index cards enough, select all the partial products and select all the ways to break apart; three right answers per kind on 2026-10-29, one on Friday 2026-10-30. Her first try of p. 51 showed four places where she thinks wrong, and the tasks and the theory lean on them: 20 × 50 written as 100 (a basic fact that already ends in 0 still gets two zeros), 10 × 18 done as (18 × 10) + (18 × 8) (each part is multiplied by the other factor), 100 + 90 + 40 + 36 added to 206 (the carry in the tens), and 28 × 30 left as a piece too big for her head (break both factors). The Lesson 4-4 theory warns about the same spots
- daily goals of a homework week: three right answers per kind on the day a homework is given, two per kind on each repeat day until its last day (`AGAIN`, owner 2026-10-29; it was one before), on 2026-10-29 that is 27 for Lesson 4-5 plus 44 for Lessons 4-2 to 4-4
- theory before the task (since 2026-10-29): a task failed last time starts with its theory and quiz in Play, the homework and the rework alike; the only way from a theory page to the task is its quiz (passed or failed), and Play, the homework or the rework button lead back to an unfinished theory or quiz. The quiz opens only after a minimum reading time: part of a full read (about 90 words a minute), random between reads so it cannot be learned, at least 20 seconds (15 for a page read before) and at most 90; pressing too early says the theory is not read yet. Progress has a Theory and quizzes card: every theory of the app, its reads in the period and all time, the time from opening the page to the quiz against a full read, the early presses and every quiz result
- memory checks (`src/engine/facts.ts`, skill `mult-facts`, since 2026-10-29): every 4th task in Play and in the homework is a multiplication fact from 0 × 0 to 9 × 9 or a number × 10, × 100 or × 1,000, marked "Memory check, from your head: no notepad, no paper" and shown without the notepad. A missed fact comes back more often until she gets it right (8 × 7 and 7 × 8 are one fact), and it is never sent to theory and a quiz; 6 to 9 come more often than the easy ones, 0 and 1 rarely. Every × 10 and × 100 task reminds her that it is just writing zeros. The theory says the same: memory, not paper, with a trick for each family (0, 1, 2, 5, 9, squares, turn it around) while a fact is still new
- test prep for Friday 2026-10-23 (home screen button, theory first, then tasks): Topic 3 "multiply by 1-digit numbers" (multiples of 10, 100 and 1,000; estimating products; mental math with properties and compensation; "select all that apply" expressions and partial products; bar diagrams and multi-step word problems with tables) and Science Chapter 2 "What affects motion?" (motion, reference point, force, gravity, relative motion, kinds of motion, balanced forces, the speed words of Part II and the point A to D map of the textbook)

Science words (`src/engine/scienceWords.ts`): fill in the missing word of the Chapter 2 study guide (parts I and II, word for word) and of sentences from her life, 30 sentences, typed (mostly, like the test) or from a word bank. The word is always English, the Russian screen adds a translation; case, spaces, an article and a full stop do not matter, the spelling does (a slip gets "Almost! Check the spelling", another science word gets what that word means). Open from the start, in test prep, the practice test and fix my mistakes

Study time (`src/engine/attention.ts`, one state machine with pure functions): the clock counts only on a learning screen, with the page visible and only the time her activity proves (touches, keys, wheel, finger moves, scrolling anywhere, notepad strokes). After 60 s without activity: "Ameliia, are you here?" (Yes / No / I don't know, a new random order for every window, buttons locked for half a second); the quiet minute is not counted. Yes runs the clock again; No or I don't know save everything (localStorage and IndexedDB) and close the tab, or show "Your progress is saved" where the browser keeps the tab. Another tab, another app or a locked screen stops the clock; coming back asks "Continue / Stop"; Stop stays on the same screen with a Continue bar. Home, settings and progress never ask. For checks: `?idleMs=5000&tickMs=1000`

Two open copies of the game (two tabs, an old tab and a new link) share one progress: a copy that comes back or sees another copy save takes the newer progress and never writes its older one over it

Code: `src/engine/` (task generators, `topic3.ts` and `forces.ts` for the test prep, `topic4.ts` for the Lesson 4-2 homework, `topic4est.ts` for Lesson 4-3, `topic4arr.ts` for Lesson 4-4, `topic4area.ts` for Lesson 4-5, theory, quizzes, game rules), `src/ui/` (Lina and Pip, long division board, area model, number line, trip map, paintings), `src/main.tsx` (screens).

`bun test tests` checks every generated task: answers recomputed independently, every equation in steps and theory checked, realistic speeds and story numbers, no repeats, the 50/25/25 time split.

## Versions

- current version: `src/` -> https://daggerok.github.io/ameliia/, its name for the day it gets archived is in `<meta name="version">` of `src/index.html`
- backups: `backups/vYYYYMMDD-suffix/` -> https://daggerok.github.io/ameliia/vYYYYMMDD-suffix/, the date is when that version was made, the suffix is one lowercase word that says what it is
- list of all of them: https://daggerok.github.io/ameliia/backups/ (dates come from folder names, titles from each page's `<title>`)

`bun run build` builds every version into `dist/` (`bun run build-github-pages` does the same with `/ameliia/` as public url)

To archive the current latest: copy `src/` into `backups/<its meta version>/`, then put the new version into `src/` with a new `<meta name="version" content="vYYYYMMDD-suffix">`

Run one backup locally: `bunx parcel backups/v20230618-addition/index.html`
